# STOP — zionsprayfoam.com collides with real, currently-operating businesses (plural)

Build paused before scaffolding, per the standing practice established after the venardsprayfoam.com
finding: for any domain reading as a name + trade, search the real name + trade combo before
building, and stop+report if a real operating business turns up under that name.

## What was found

Searching "zion spray foam" / "zion insulators" turns up at least two distinct, real, currently
operating businesses in this exact trade:

**1. Zion Insulators** — fully established, multi-source-verified business:
- BBB Business Profile: "Insulation Contractors in Williamsburg, VA" — 484 Penniman Road,
  Williamsburg, VA 23185
- Yelp listing (40 photos): 919 E Main St, Ste 1000, Richmond, VA 23225
- Phone: (757) 842-0846 (consistent across Yelp, Mapquest, BBB)
- Self-description: "Zion Insulators dominates the area with Spray Foam Insulation needs to
  customers. We are a Veteran Owned company specializing in attic and crawl space cleanup..."
- This is not a borderline case — BBB profile + Yelp + Mapquest + consistent phone number across
  all three is a fully real, operating, veteran-owned insulation contractor in the Richmond/
  Williamsburg, VA area.

**2. Zion Sprayfoam** — active Facebook business page, Kansas City, Missouri ("Zion Sprayfoam is
on Facebook... Kansas City, MO. Lives in Kansas City, Missouri"). Name matches the domain almost
exactly (zionsprayfoam.com ↔ "Zion Sprayfoam").

**3. Possible secondary signal (unconfirmed):** a business-registry aggregator (b2bhint.com)
surfaced a reference to "Zion Spray Foam, Inc." tied to a Virginia State Corporation Commission
filing, "same officer" as an entity called "Haro's Spray Foam Insulation, Inc." A direct follow-up
search for "Zion Spray Foam Inc Virginia" returned no results, so this one is NOT independently
confirmed the way #1 and #2 are — noting it for completeness, not treating it as verified.

## Why this stops the build

Two independently, multi-source-verified real businesses (Zion Insulators in VA; Zion Sprayfoam
in Kansas City) already operate in the exact trade this domain would be built for. Per the
standing rule from the venardsprayfoam.com finding, this is not a generic-term situation to route
around — building "zionsprayfoam.com" as a named spray-foam-contractor lead-gen brand would read
as a lookalike/soundalike site for either or both of these real operators, in their own trade,
under essentially their own name.

## Recommendation

Do not build. Flagging to Josh directly per standing instruction, same as venardsprayfoam.com.

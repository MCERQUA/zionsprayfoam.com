# Competitor Analysis — zionsprayfoam.com

Source: DataForSEO SERP (organic, St. George UT / Utah, depth 15) for "spray foam insulation st
george utah" and "zion spray foam utah", 2026-08-20.

County with several genuine local installers plus national franchise/aggregator listings.

## Gaps we exploit
1. **No dedicated Zion-gateway-region brand.** Existing competitors brand around Southern Utah
   generally (so-utah-sprayfoam.com), a specific city (Fox Foam/Hurricane), or a generic company
   name (Big City, Red Knoll) — none lean into the Zion National Park gateway identity itself,
   which is a strong, recognizable regional hook for a business actually based in/serving that
   corridor (St. George, Hurricane, Springdale, Washington, Ivins, Santa Clara).
2. **Metal building / pole barn spray foam is a validated distinct service line** (confirmed by
   sprayfoamstgeorgeut.com specializing in exactly this) — worth its own dedicated cluster page
   rather than folding into general "residential" copy, matching real local demand patterns
   (agricultural/rural pole barns and metal shops are common in this part of Utah).
3. **Insulation removal/repair** carries real, low-competition, high-CPC demand ($11.55 CPC,
   LOW competition nationally) that none of the directly-listed local competitors appear to lead
   with as a named service — an underserved angle worth its own page.
4. **Cost/education content gap.** PAA signals ("How much does it cost to have spray foam
   insulation installed?", "Does spray foam insulation lower home value?") aren't directly
   answered by any of the local competitor pages found — real content opportunity for blog posts.

## What we will NOT do
- Will not use "Zion Spray Foam" in a way that could be confused with the two real, unrelated
  "Zion"-named spray foam businesses identified elsewhere (Zion Insulators, Richmond/Williamsburg
  VA; Zion Sprayfoam, Kansas City MO) — this site is explicitly geographically scoped to the Zion
  National Park / southwestern Utah gateway region, which is a different, real, and verified-clear
  market from both of those companies' actual service areas. Escalated to Josh via email per
  standing practice before proceeding; approved to build with this scoping.
- Will not chase the massive national head term "spray foam insulation" (90,500/mo) — far too
  broad/competitive for a single-location local installer; hyperlocal terms and the validated
  service-line pages are the real opportunity.

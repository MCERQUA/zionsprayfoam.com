Common contact-form questions include whether a specific property type (metal building, existing home, new construction) fits our services, whether open-cell or closed-cell foam makes more sense for a specific application, and general questions about how the quoting and installation process works. If your question fits somewhere in that range, send it over — we're happy to give a straight, specific answer rather than a generic one.

## Response Time

We aim to respond to contact form messages within one business day. For a time-sensitive project or an active moisture/insulation failure issue, calling directly is the fastest way to get a response.

If you already know your property type and which space needs attention, requesting a quote directly will usually get you an answer faster than a general contact message, since it routes straight to project-specific information instead of a back-and-forth to figure out the basics first.

The Zion Gateway Climate

Living in St. George, Hurricane, or Springdale means battling wild temperature fluctuations. A standard vented attic acts as an oven in July and a freezer in January. Spray foam creates an unyielding thermal envelope.

mode_heat

Desert Heat Gain

During peak St. George summers, attic temperatures can exceed 150°F. This radiant heat presses down into your living space, forcing your HVAC to run continuously. Spray foam blocks radiant heat transfer, keeping the attic within 10-15 degrees of your indoor temperature.

  • check_circle Lower cooling costs
  • check_circle Extended HVAC lifespan
ac_unit

High-Desert Winter Loss

The clear desert skies that bring winter sun also cause rapid heat loss at night. Traditional fiberglass batts allow warm air to escape through convection. Our closed-cell foam creates an absolute air barrier, trapping your expensive heated air inside.

  • check_circle Stop winter drafts
  • check_circle Consistent room temperatures
